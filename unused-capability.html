The case

What goes unused when a room lets only one kind of mind show

A talent shows up when the environment lets it. When it does not, the organisation pays for capability it never gets.

This is TƎT's proposal, not a measured result. The page says which parts are which.

Talents depend on the room

TƎT synthesis, with an illustration

TƎT names eight talents, so far: integrative thinking, creative implementation, strategic empathy, cognitive dexterity, embodiment, deep focus, emotional intelligence and regulation, and adaptive masking. Each starts as friction: a behaviour that is useful and still looks excessive or out of place.

A talent describes what an environment lets someone do. It is not a trait, a type or a score. People with cognitively strong profiles are the ones for whom the gap between what they can do and what a room allows tends to be widest.

In the Antwerp Maritime Academy case, students began saying “this needs someone to hold the question open.” The same behaviour, once named, became a contribution to the task.

Three ways capability goes unused

Hypothesis

  • Hidden. People spend effort translating themselves for the room, and that effort leaves less for the work.
  • Misread. The same behaviour is punished in one room and prized in another. Where it is punished, it reads as excess, not as a contribution.
  • Unnamed. A capability nobody can explain to a manager stays privately known and institutionally invisible.

TƎT proposes these as a way to look at a team. We have not measured them.

Why now

Hypothesis

TƎT proposes that Europe's twin digital and green transitions, on top of compounding crises, raise the demand for complex reasoning, and that institutions built for stability waste it. We have not yet attached independent evidence to this argument.

What we can and cannot show

  • Some evidence: a sustained effort to fit a room carries personal costs, as the research on the translation page describes. It is emerging evidence from one group, not a general finding.
  • No evidence yet: that organisations measurably lose output this way, or how much.
  • A real weak spot: some of what looks like unused capability may simply be absent. From outside, we cannot always tell the two apart.

Boundaries

  • TƎT is not used to select, rate, rank or assess individuals.
  • TƎT does not replace any adjustment a person is entitled to.
  • A talent is never a label for a person.
